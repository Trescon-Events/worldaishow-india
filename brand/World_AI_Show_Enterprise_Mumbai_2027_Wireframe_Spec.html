02Brand kit & design direction

The visual identity incorporates the master brand guidelines established for the World AI Show platform[cite: 31]. It projects an authoritative, high-density, luminous enterprise feel engineered for enterprise CXOs, operational leaders, and technical buyers[cite: 1, 31].

Tone of the design

The audience consists of CIOs, Chief AI Officers, CTOs, CDOs, CISOs, enterprise architects, and transformation heads[cite: 1]. The visual aesthetic must convey enterprise-grade reliability and architectural rigor rather than speculative consumer excitement[cite: 1, 31].

DoDeep obsidian backgrounds (#020814), glass cards with subtle borders, clean mono labels, high-contrast Cyber Lime CTA pills with dark ink, and verified evidence metrics[cite: 31].
AvoidGeneric purple-to-blue consumer gradients, floating sci-fi robot hands, stock circuit boards, ungrounded futurism, and countdown pressure timers[cite: 31, 33].

Rule — Button Ink Guardrail

Never place white text inside a Cyber Lime (#C0F43C) button or badge[cite: 31]. White on lime yields an unreadable 1.3:1 contrast ratio[cite: 31]. Typography inside Cyber Lime elements must strictly use Carbon Ink (#06111F !important)[cite: 31].
