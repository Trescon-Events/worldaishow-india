12. Brand Governance & Guardrails

Strict rules for preserving the integrity, legibility, and premium perception of the World AI Show brand.

✓ DO: Use Dark Carbon Ink on Cyber Lime
Correct Contrast (#06111F Ink) ✓

Always render typography inside Cyber Lime elements using #06111F.

✕ DON'T: Place White Text on Cyber Lime
Unreadable White Text (Violates WCAG) ✕

Never put white text on lime. The resulting 1.3:1 contrast ratio is completely illegible.

✓ DO: Maintain Original 2.03:1 Aspect Ratio

Scale uniformly holding the Shift key or preserving SVG viewBox proportions.

✕ DON'T: Horizontally or Vertically Skew

Never stretch, condense, skew, rotate, or alter vector kerning.
